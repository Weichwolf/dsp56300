CMPM magnitude boundaries

DSP56300FM Rev.5 page13-47 defines CMPM as |S2|-|S1| on 56-bit operands;
table5-1 defines the condition codes. Magnitudes include +2^55, so the sign
bit of a magnitude is not its mathematical sign. The difference 0-|min56|
is representable and does not set V/L; |min56|-0 is positive overflow;
two minima compare equal. Ordinary signed CMP retains its separate rules.

The implementation computes magnitude subtraction independently of signed
addition overflow. Accumulators remain unchanged, status S/L stays sticky,
and the existing scaling/parallel-publication mechanisms remain in force.
This is the declared mathematical/manual interpretation. The minimum-operand
flags have not been independently captured from silicon. CMPM carry is only
qualified for properly sign-extended destinations, as stated in the manual.

The boundary test contains 15 literal normal/down/up flag triples, including
ordinary signed-CMP controls. Both accumulators, four initial CCR patterns,
three execution backends and dispatched/owned-fetch/captured paths give 3240
cases. It checks complete architectural state, one-word nominal cost and the
exact declared opcode-bus traffic. General arithmetic, flag sequences, staged
publication and saturation retain their separate regression coverage.

  cmake --build --preset strict --target dsp56300_magnitude_compare_test
  ctest --preset strict -R '^magnitude-compare-boundaries$' --output-on-failure

Repeat with the thread preset for TSan. These tests do not establish silicon
sampling times, complete ISA correctness, device startup or realtime speed.
