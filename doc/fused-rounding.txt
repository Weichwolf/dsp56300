Fused rounded product flags

MACR overflow describes its widened, rounded result. At the signed56 negative
boundary, accumulation can wrap negative-to-positive and rounding can wrap
positive-to-negative, leaving a representable final result. OR-ing those two
isolated overflows incorrectly sets V and sticky L. Their XOR gives the final
overflow; actual subsequent SM limiting still sets V/L, and incoming L survives.
Standalone MAC and RND retain their separate, genuine overflow behavior.

Example (fractional signed operands, scale down, either RM):
  A = $80000000000000; X0 = 1; Y0 = $800000
  MACR X0,Y0,A => A unchanged, no new V/L
The unrounded MAC and RND of its wrapped $7FFFFFFF000000 result are controls
that each set V/L. No instruction, result or firmware routine is bypassed.

General execution calculates flags once after fused rounding. Native x64 uses
XOR instead of OR at the existing overflow combination, with no additional
instruction/helper, admission change, public header or checkpoint-layout change.

The 5185-case producer uses widened signed integer products and independent
quotient/remainder rounding. Literal tests check full state, exact progress,
ordinary/bounded native execution, all six publication policies and absence of
an erroneous staged L writer. The published preceding archive fails the new
fixture at the intended flag boundary while producing the right data word.

A private software comparison uses 120 independently authored inputs: five
fused/unrounded/isolated-round/no-wrap/zero-product patterns, both accumulators,
three scales, both RM values and SM clear/set. All initial register readbacks
match; all 360 final reference/cached/JIT observations match sim563.dll. This
is manufacturer-software evidence, not physical-chip validation. The simulator
binary, raw observations and program words remain local; only hashes are public.

An independent SMT reduction checks every signed56 accumulator and even product
term in [-2^47,+2^47], encompassing signed fractional 24-bit products, for each
of the three rounding granularities and both policies. All three XOR equivalence
queries are UNSAT; the old OR has a concrete counterexample. SM limiting is
separate. Mathematical equivalence does not prove silicon behavior or timing.

Exactly 224 existing immediate/power fixture cases change expected V/L only;
all inputs, programs, data words and other bytes are identical. The native SM
regression matrix now uses independent per-bit truth tables for all 26 logical
forms already admitted by the published core, retaining every input and checking
actual helper-free full state instead of expecting an obsolete rejection.
Existing mixed
and saturation archives remain byte-identical. No ISA row becomes complete.
Full numerical-mode, pipeline/interrupt and connected-instrument qualification
remain open. Counts, source pins and sanitizer scope are in
fused-rounding-reference.json; no throughput factor is claimed by this fix.
