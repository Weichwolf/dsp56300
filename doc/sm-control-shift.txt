SM control transfers and shifted accumulator arithmetic

DSP56300FM Rev.5 sections 3.1.5/3.2.3 and the instruction diagrams define
arithmetic limiting separately from control-register transfers. MOVEC can
replace SR while SM is already set. The qualified SR forms are long immediate,
X/Y absolute short and X/Y absolute long, in both memory directions.

ADDL/ADDR/SUBL/SUBR use the existing generic shift/add/subtract arithmetic and
its table 3-1 limiter. Their SM admission is now explicit. Full-width carry,
shift overflow, sticky flags and scaling retain their separate meanings.
C after an overflowing initial left shift remains outside the manual's
qualified domain; the test excludes only that predeclared C observation.

The two new matrices contain 2187 control cases and 10368 shifted arithmetic
cases across reference/cached/JIT dispatch and genuine owned/captured staged
execution. They compare complete architectural registers and stack, literal
instruction lengths/base costs and exact program/operand bus transactions.
Control RAM callbacks read/write the declared test buffers; absent transactions
are rejected. Shift expectations use signed integer division/range arithmetic
and the independent three-bit limiting table, not the core's aligned formulas.

The original ADDL/ADDR unsupported-SM stimuli retain their inputs and now check
full independently calculated successful outcomes. Other unqualified modes
retain their failures. Final-source negative controls linked to the prior 0115
archive reproduce both missing domains. The reference manifest pins the
sanitizer profiles, tests and unchanged arithmetic/addition/flags/saturation
archives. This qualifies named forms, not all SM/ISA modes, native lowering,
pipeline/IRQ timing, throughput or a complete instrument.
