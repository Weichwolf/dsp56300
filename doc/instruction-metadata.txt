Pure instruction shape inspection

Include <dsp56300/instruction.hpp> and link dsp56300::core. The free noexcept
inspect_instruction(Word) returns optional<InstructionShape>. It constructs no
Core, reads no emulated memory, performs no I/O/fetch/acknowledgement and advances
no clock or CPU state. It uses the independent opcode decoder directly.

Field        Meaning
kind         Recognized encoded family; not every operand/mode is qualified
words        One or two captured words required by that recognized shape
repeatable   One-word family structurally eligible as a REP body, if otherwise legal
nullopt      An over-24-bit input or an opcode with no recognized shape

Inputs are never silently masked into a different 24-bit opcode. Structural
recognition does not validate all register selectors, addressing combinations,
ALU forms or numerical modes. It must not manufacture a successful execution.
The native executor remains responsible for actual operands, effects and faults.
A caller with an unknown shape must report context rather than guess its width
or perform speculative extension reads. The query supplies no extension value,
physical bus receipt, calibrated cost or dependency/forwarding mask.

For example, independently encoded MOVE X:(R0)+,X0 (44d800) is one word;
MOVE #extension,X0 (44f400) and MOVEM P:extension,X0 (07f084) are two words.
The latter cannot become a REP body merely because their families allow REP.
Tests also specify immediate/register loops, short/effective branches/calls,
returns, RESET/WAIT/STOP, ILLEGAL/TRAP and relative cache controls independently.

Directed qualification: 19 literal forms plus three unknown/width rejection
cases, 41 assertions under ASan/UBSan and separate TSan. Only the two new query/
test C++ contexts compile; the 59 prior CPU contexts retain exact source/object/
recipe identities. At d7429d3, the qualified archive has 60 instrumented members
and the query executable retains strict hardening. No fuzzing, manufacturer programs
or application programs are used.

  cmake --preset strict
  cmake --build build/strict --target dsp56300_instruction_shape_test -j2
  ctest --test-dir build/strict -R '^instruction-shapes$' --output-on-failure

Use the thread preset/build directory for the separate TSan scope. These tests
do not establish complete ISA, exact operand dependencies, early AGU/MOVE
publication, overlapped execution, calibrated pipeline/IRQ/bus timing, staged
native JIT throughput or complete device operation. Those requirements remain
open. Compiler-conservative all-bank masks are not exact physical dependencies.
