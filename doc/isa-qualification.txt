Complete DSP56300 ISA qualification

Complete support and independent tests are a mandatory completion criterion.
The current implementation is partial. A passing CTest suite and successful
Blofeld/FFT routines prove only their tested subsets. Performance work does not
waive missing instructions, operand forms, numerical modes or observable effects.

doc/isa-coverage.tsv inventories the 117 distinct entries in the DSP56300FM
Rev.5 instruction summary (including MOVE subforms and mixed multiply forms),
plus the LEA alias identified with LUA in appendix A. These are catalogue rows,
not a count of disjoint opcodes or a percentage of full ISA support. The table
contains original project assessments and source/test pointers, not copied
manual descriptions or manufacturer programs. Detailed format expansion is
still required; a mnemonic-level list alone cannot prove encoding coverage.

Reference: DSP56300 Family Manual, Rev.5 (April 2005), chapters 3/4/5/12/13
and appendix A. Copies/extracts and private analysis remain in doc/local/.
Manufacturer URL: https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/reference-manual/DSP56300FM.pdf

Statuses:
  missing: no implementation for this instruction/family/form.
  untested: code exists, but dedicated independent qualification is absent.
  partial: linked tests cover some semantics/forms; the listed gaps remain.
  complete: all required forms/modes/effects independently qualified; no gaps.
No entry currently claims complete support. Existence of a test source is
evidence to inspect, not proof that every row/form it mentions is covered.

Audit commands:
  python3 tools/isa_audit.py
  python3 tools/isa_audit.py --require-complete
The first validates catalogue structure/evidence paths and reports the backlog.
The second deliberately fails while any row is incomplete. It is an additional
necessary completion gate, not a substitute for the semantic witnesses below.
It is not hidden behind a passing current-subset CTest result.

Before marking an entry complete, expand and qualify:
- Every documented encoding, extension width, condition and legal operand set;
  reserved selectors/opcodes remain explicitly distinguished from valid forms.
- All legal numerical modes/combinations, precision, rounding, saturation,
  flags written/preserved and old-input ordering with every parallel move class.
- Addressing and aliases, word/address wrap, short/long/immediate forms,
  register-field effects and fault/transfer boundaries without duplicate I/O.
- REP, DO/DOR/FOREVER/ENDDO/BRKcc, nesting, subroutines, stack/extension behavior,
  exact partial budgets and admitted IRQ/exception sequences.
- Core cache/control/STOP/WAIT/RESET/debug/trap behavior with explicit host/event
  contracts. Such instructions cannot be silently replaced by NOPs or invented
  peripheral replies just to pass a program.
- Documented instruction costs and architectural sequence restrictions; retain
  explicit limitations for uncalibrated pipeline/external-bus timing.
- Reproducible independently modeled binary fixtures and boundary/interaction
  tests on reference, cached and native backends in release and ASan/UBSan.
  Differential agreement alone cannot prove correctness of shared semantics.

Current closure increment: INC/DEC now change the raw accumulator's least
significant bit (not its A1/B1 word), and CMPU compares zero-extended unsigned
48-bit operands while ignoring accumulator EXP bytes. CMPU preserves S/L/E/U,
clears V, uses arithmetic bit 55 for N, and tests low 48 bits for Z. Arithmetic
saturation is explicitly bypassed for CMPU as specified in section 3.2.3.
These additions still do not close all numerical modes or full ISA qualification.
