Original DSP emulator and reused blofeld-emu project setup: GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE).

Project setup (CMake quality/hardening, presets and Clang configuration) originates in
https://github.com/Weichwolf/blofeld-emu, revision
2978c190143ef757189b3b82eb0e9350720eb954. No board emulation is copied.
The DSP emulator has no dependency on another DSP emulator or JIT library.

Manufacturer programs, tables, manuals and Blofeld source extracts are private
reference inputs. They are not part of this library or its redistributable tests.
