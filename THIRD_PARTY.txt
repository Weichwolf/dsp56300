LLVM 19 compiler support:
  https://llvm.org/
  Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions; full notice: LICENSES/LLVM.txt.
  The cold optimizing compiler uses the installed shared LLVM development package
  through its supported C API. No LLVM source is built, patched or instrumented.
  LLVM does not supply the guest ISA implementation or device behavior.

Manufacturer programs and source extracts are not used by this project's tests
or benchmarks. All distributed DSP test programs are authored independently.
Manufacturer manuals are reference material only and stay outside the tracked tree.
